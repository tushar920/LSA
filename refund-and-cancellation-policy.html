Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Terms & ConditionsPrivacy PolicyRefund & Cancellation Policy

This Refund and Cancellation Policy applies to pickleball court bookings made with Pickle Pros Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us", "our"), operating under the brand Legaxy Sports Academy/Pickle Pros, through https://legaxysportsacademy.com ("Website") or at our venue. Registered office: House No. 560, Sector-9, Gurgaon, Sadar Bazar, Haryana, India, 122001.

By booking a court you agree to this policy.

1. Cancellation by the customer

The refund depends on how long before the booked slot start time we receive your cancellation request.

When you cancelRefund
More than 24 hours before the slot100% of the booking amount
Between 24 hours and 6 hours before the slot50% of the booking amount
Less than 6 hours before the slotNo refund
No-show or late arrivalNo refund

To cancel, write to hello@legaxy.org or call +91 9667084900 with your name, booking ID, and slot date and time. The time we receive your request decides the refund slab.

2. Rescheduling

  1. You may reschedule a booking once, free of charge, if you ask at least 6 hours before the slot start time.
  2. Rescheduling depends on court availability. The new slot must fall within 30 days of the original slot.
  3. If the new slot costs more, you pay the difference. If it costs less, the difference is not refunded.
  4. A rescheduled booking cannot be cancelled for a refund.

3. Cancellation by us

We may cancel or cut short a booking because of rain or unsafe weather, power failure, court maintenance, government orders, or any other reason beyond our reasonable control. In that case you may choose either of the following:

  • a full refund of the affected booking, or a pro-rata refund for the unused playing time; or
  • a replacement slot of the same duration at no extra cost.

4. Late arrival and unused time

Your slot starts and ends at the booked time. Playing time lost because you arrived late or left early is not extended, carried forward, or refunded.

5. Non-refundable items

The following are not refundable:

  • bookings cancelled less than 6 hours before the slot, and no-shows;
  • bookings ended early by us because of misconduct or breach of our venue rules;
  • charges for equipment rental once the equipment has been issued;
  • payment gateway or convenience fees charged by third parties, where applicable;
  • discounts, promotional credits and complimentary slots, which have no cash value.

6. Failed or duplicate payments

If your account is debited but the booking is not confirmed, or you are charged twice for the same booking, write to us with the transaction reference. We will verify the payment and refund the extra amount in full.

7. How refunds are paid

  1. Approved refunds are initiated within 5 to 7 working days of approval.
  2. Refunds go to the original payment method. Your bank or card issuer may take additional time to credit the amount.
  3. For bookings paid in cash at the venue, refunds are made by UPI or bank transfer to the account you give us.
  4. Refunds are made in Indian Rupees only.
