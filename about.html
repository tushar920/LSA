Our Mission

Spot. Train. Rise.

We believe every child deserves access to world-class sports education — regardless of school size, city, or infrastructure. Our mission is to identify latent talent early, nurture it systematically, and build pathways for students to rise to their potential.

LegaXy SSEP (School Sports Education Program) is a fully managed, end-to-end sports infrastructure that plugs into a school's existing structure — coaches, curriculum, reporting, events — all owned and operated by us.

Our Journey

How LegaXy Was Born

LegaXy was founded on a simple, frustrating observation: India produces extraordinary athletes, yet most schools deliver sport as an afterthought. We set out to fix that — systemically.

2022

The Idea

LegaXy founded with a vision to build India's first truly managed, athlete-led school sports program.

2023

SSEP Framework Built

The School Sports Education Program curriculum was designed — a 36-week, age-specific framework covering Classes 1–10.

2024

Elite Athletes Join

Partnered with Yuzvendra Chahal, Ishant Sharma, Puja Tomar, and other elite Indian athletes as program mentors.

2025–

Scaling India

Expanding SSEP across schools in Delhi NCR and beyond — building the sports culture India's students deserve.

Our Approach

Most school sports fail because they're fragmented — one PE teacher, no curriculum, no reporting, no events. LegaXy brings the whole package: qualified coaches, age-appropriate curriculum, digital reporting, inter-school events, and parent visibility. All under one roof.
