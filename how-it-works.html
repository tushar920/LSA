The LegaXy Process

Four Steps. One Outcome.

From baseline to breakthrough — every student moves through the same proven framework, executed consistently by our certified coaches.

01
🔍

Assess — Know Every Student

Before a single session begins, we run a comprehensive baseline evaluation covering each student's fitness, coordination, and sport-specific skill levels. This data shapes everything downstream — ensuring coaching is targeted, not generic.

What the Baseline Covers

Physical fitness markers (speed, strength, flexibility), coordination tests, sport aptitude, and a wellness intake. All data is captured in the LegaXy app and benchmarked against national age norms.

Fitness TestingSkill BaselineData CaptureAge Benchmarks
02
🏋️

Train — Structured Sessions, Expert Coaches

Age-appropriate, curriculum-aligned 80-minute sessions led by certified coaches. Every session follows the LegaXy Training Blueprint — a five-phase structure designed for maximum development and engagement.

The LegaXy Training Blueprint

Every session follows our proven 5-phase structure: dynamic warm-up → skill focus → drill progression → team/match play → cool-down & debrief. No two sessions are identical — each follows the 36-week annual curriculum.

80-Minute SessionsCertified Coaches36-Week Curriculum5-Phase Blueprint
03
📈

Track — Data That Means Something

We continuously measure progress against each student's own baseline. Schools get periodic reports, parents get visibility via the LegaXy app, and coaches use data to refine sessions in real time.

What Gets Tracked

Attendance, skill scores across 12+ parameters, fitness benchmarks, coach notes, injury logs, and tournament participation. All visible to schools, parents, and LegaXy management in real time.

LegaXy AppSkill ReportsParent DashboardPeriodic Reports
04
🏆

Compete — Where Growth Becomes Real

Students get inter-school exposure and competitive opportunities — School Olympics, inter-house tournaments, and external circuits. Competition builds character, teaches resilience, and makes progress visible to the student themselves.

Competitive Ecosystem

LegaXy manages a full competitive calendar — inter-house events, inter-school leagues, Parents Premier League, and School Olympics. Students compete at the right level for their development stage.

Session Design

What 80 Minutes Looks Like

Every LegaXy session follows the same five-phase blueprint — designed for maximum development, engagement, and safety.

10 min
Phase 1
Dynamic Warm-Up
Mobility, activation, and sport-specific movement prep
20 min
Phase 2
Skill Focus
Curriculum-aligned technical skill instruction and demonstration
20 min
Phase 3
Drill Progression
Progressive drills building from isolated skill to game-like scenarios
20 min
Phase 4
Match Play
Structured small-sided games or full matches with tactical guidance
10 min
Phase 5
Cool-Down & Debrief
Recovery, reflection, and session data capture in the LegaXy app
