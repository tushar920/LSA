Our Philosophy

Why Multi-Sport Before Specialisation?

Research consistently shows that early sport specialisation increases injury risk and burnout, while multi-sport athletes develop better movement literacy, decision-making, and long-term performance. We expose students to 8+ sports before guiding them toward specialisation in Classes 8–10.

🔄

Cross-Sport Transfer

Skills learned in one sport enhance performance in others — agility from badminton, vision from football, coordination from TT.

🎯

Talent Discovery

Students often don't know which sport suits them best. Broad exposure reveals hidden strengths we'd never find otherwise.

💪

Reduced Burnout

Variety keeps motivation high. Students stay engaged, excited, and come back — because every term brings something new.

Curriculum Structure

Sports by Class Band

Sports are rotated across the year within each class band — ensuring every student gets structured exposure at the right developmental stage.

Foundation Stage

Classes 1–3

Ages 6–9 Years

Fitness & Movement, Multi-Sport Play, Basic Ball Skills, Balance Games. Focus on joy, exploration, and physical literacy.

Development Stage

Classes 4–7

Ages 9–12 Years

Cricket, Football, Basketball, Badminton, Table Tennis, Self-Defense. Multi-sport sampling with structured technique introduction.

Performance Stage

Classes 8–10

Ages 12–16 Years

Advanced specialisation pathways, all core sports plus Pickleball & Swimming. Competitive exposure and inter-school tournaments.
