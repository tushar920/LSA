Common Questions

What Schools Ask Us

We already have a PE teacher — do we still need LegaXy? +
Yes — and your PE teacher will thank you for it. LegaXy doesn't replace your teacher, we give them a structured framework, certified coaches, equipment, and reporting tools they didn't have before. Most schools find their PE teacher becomes far more effective with our system in place.
How does LegaXy fit into our existing timetable? +
We design the SSEP around your schedule — whether that's a 40-minute period or an 80-minute block. Our team works with your academic coordinator to fit seamlessly into available slots without disrupting core subjects.
What if we don't have proper sports facilities? +
That's fine — we start from wherever you are. We conduct a facility assessment during onboarding and design programs around your available space. We bring all equipment and can run effective sessions on open grounds, covered halls, or multi-purpose courts.
How do parents know what's happening in sports? +
Every parent gets visibility via the LegaXy app — their child's attendance, skill progress, fitness benchmarks, upcoming events, and coach notes. Sports becomes a transparent, reportable part of school life, not a black box.
What does the school actually need to do? +
Very little. Provide access to available space during allocated periods, and basic student roster information for baseline assessments. Everything else — coaches, equipment, curriculum, events, reporting — is owned and operated by LegaXy.
What does it cost? +
Pricing is customised based on school size, number of classes, sports selected, and program type. We structure it to be sustainable and scale with you. Book a free call and we'll give you a transparent proposal with no hidden costs.
