Common Questions

FAQs for Schools

How does LegaXy integrate with our existing timetable? +
We design the SSEP around your existing schedule — whether that's a 40-minute period or an 80-minute block. Our team works with your academic coordinator to fit into available slots without disrupting core subjects.
Do we need existing sports infrastructure? +
No. We conduct a facility assessment as part of onboarding and design programs around what's available. We bring all necessary equipment and can work with any space — from large grounds to covered halls.
How are coaches vetted and trained? +
LegaXy coaches are certified sport-specific professionals who go through our internal LegaXy Coach Certification before deployment. All coaches are background verified, trained in child safety protocols, and supervised by our regional sports managers.
What does the school need to provide? +
Access to available outdoor or indoor space during allocated periods, and basic student roster and health information for baseline assessments. That's it — we handle everything else, including equipment, coaches, reporting, and events.
What's the typical contract length? +
LegaXy SSEP programs run on an academic year basis (12 months), with 36-week delivery aligned to the school calendar. Multi-year partnerships with scaling options are available for schools looking to expand across programs.
