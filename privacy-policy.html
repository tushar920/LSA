We handle personal data in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules, as they apply. By using the Website or our services, you agree to this policy.

2. Why we use your personal data

We use your personal data to:

  • create and manage your booking and account;
  • collect payments and process cancellations and refunds;
  • send booking confirmations, reminders and service messages by SMS, email or WhatsApp;
  • answer your questions and resolve complaints;
  • keep the venue safe and secure, and deal with incidents;
  • improve the Website and our services;
  • meet legal, tax and accounting requirements;
  • send offers and updates, only where you have agreed to receive them.

3. Consent and withdrawal

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, or for other uses the law permits, such as meeting a legal obligation. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us at the contact details in clause 12. If you withdraw consent, we may not be able to provide bookings or other services that need that data. You can opt out of promotional messages at any time by following the instruction in the message or by writing to us.

4. Who we share personal data with

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with:

  • payment gateways, banks and payment processors, to complete payments and refunds;
  • service providers who work for us, such as booking software, website hosting, SMS, email and WhatsApp messaging, and accounting providers, under confidentiality obligations;
  • government authorities, courts and law enforcement agencies, where the law requires it;
  • professional advisers, auditors and insurers, where needed;
  • a buyer or successor, if our business is merged, sold or reorganised.

5. Cookies

The Website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep the Website working, remember your preferences and understand how visitors use it. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the Website may not work properly without them.

6. How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy or as the law requires. Booking and payment records are kept for the period required under tax and accounting laws. CCTV footage is normally kept for up to 30 days, unless needed for an investigation or a legal claim. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and restricted staff access. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • confirm what personal data we hold about you and give you a summary of it;
  • correct, complete or update your personal data;
  • erase personal data that we no longer need to keep;
  • record your withdrawal of consent;
  • register a nominee who can exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated;
  • resolve a grievance about how we handle your personal data.

To use these rights, or to delete your account, write to the Grievance Officer in clause 12. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Children

A parent or legal guardian must make bookings for anyone under 18 years of age and give consent for that child's personal data. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children without such consent. We do not track children's behaviour or target advertising at them.

10. Third-party links

The Website may link to third-party websites and services, including payment pages and social media. Their privacy practices are their own, and we are not responsible for them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Please review it periodically.
